✦ SIGIL CLASH Elemental Card Duel · offline · original

How to play

Both duelists start at 30 life. Each turn your mana refills and grows by 1, up to 10. Play cards, then End Turn — the rival plays, and the duel goes on until one sigil breaks.

Elements: ▲ Ember beats ◆ Thorn, ◆ Thorn beats ● Tide, ● Tide beats ▲ Ember. ★ Storm is wild — it beats nothing and nothing beats it.

Aura: the element of the last card you played is your aura. A strike whose element beats the rival aura deals +3 (SUPER); one it loses to deals −1.

Combo: the second card you play of one element in a turn gains +2 power.

Cards: ⚔ Strike damages, 🛡 Guard raises shield (absorbs damage first), ⇪ Surge draws cards, ✚ Mend restores life. Empty deck? Drawing costs 2 fatigue.

Keys: 1–7 play a card · Space ends the turn · Enter starts · R restarts.

Sigil Clash — an original card game. No network, no accounts, no tracking.
